## Why are there different packages?

Not everyone uses VRChat. Some people only need a 3D model for streaming apps (VSeeFace, Warudo, 3tene...), some only need VRChat, and some need both. Each platform requires a different technical setup (VRChat uses its own SDK features such as VRC PhysBones and the Expression Menu; streaming apps use the VRM standard). Different purposes mean different costs.

## Package A — Streaming apps only (VSeeFace, Warudo, 3tene...)

# PART III – TERMS OF SERVICE (TOS)

## 1. Workflow

- I livestream my work on YouTube for transparency. You can choose to allow this or not.
- **Before you pay the deposit, we confirm the scope in writing:** package, target apps (VRChat, VNyan, Warudo...), number of outfits/toggles, and number of blendshapes. Anything outside the confirmed scope counts as a new request and will be quoted separately before I start on it.
- I send progress updates **every Sunday at 18:00 (Vietnam time, GMT+7)**.
- **Shape approval checkpoint:** before texturing, I send you the untextured model to approve its shape. After approval, any change to the shape counts as a major change (see Section 6), because it means redoing the textures as well.

## 2. References

- 3 views are required: front, side, and back, so I can build the model accurately.
- Close-up images of details such as the eyes and accessories.
- If you don't have a back view, you can describe it for me, or I can design that part myself. In this case the 15% missing-reference fee in Section 4 applies (the same fee as for missing color codes, not an additional one).
- **Note:** my service is **converting an existing reference**. It does not include designing a character from scratch.
- The 3D model will follow your reference as closely as possible but **cannot match it 100%**. This is a general technical limit of 2D-to-3D conversion: shading follows the 3D shader and cannot reproduce the original hand-drawn linework, and head/body proportions may be adjusted slightly to work with the rig and animations.
- Some parts with complex shapes (tails, hair, separate accessories...) may be **simplified in shape** so the rig and physics work reliably. This is a technical limit that applies to every order; I will tell you in advance which parts are affected.
- If you provide several references that differ from each other (for example, face proportions in the turnaround vs. the Live2D art), I will ask you which one takes priority before starting, instead of guessing.
- If you have the Live2D PSD file, I will use it as a detailed reference for each part (eyes, hair, accessories...). The work is still a conversion from 2D artwork, not a direct conversion from the Live2D rig.

## 3. Copyright

- You must confirm that you are the rightful owner of the design (the original artist, or someone who has purchased usage/commercial rights to it).
- If the original artist's terms prohibit 3D derivatives, I **will not accept** the commission.
- If you are unsure about your rights, I recommend checking with the original artist before paying the deposit. I may ask for proof (purchase link, confirmation message from the artist...) before starting.
- You are responsible for the legality of using the original artwork; I am only responsible for the 3D work I create.

## 4. General Technical Terms (all packages)

- Color codes (hex) for skin, hair, eyes, and each part of the outfit, so the colors stay true to the original design.
- Standard blendshape set: 5 mouth shapes (a-i-u-e-o) + 4 expressions. Extra shapes: **$2 per shape**.
- **Missing reference fee (15% of package price):** applies when you don't provide enough color codes and/or clear shape references (side, back...) for one or more parts, so I have to judge the colors or design the shapes myself. The fee is a percentage of the original package price and applies whether color, shape, or both are missing, because both take extra time and carry a risk of revisions if you're not happy with the result. Example: if your Live2D art only shows the front, the color codes can be taken from it, but the side/back shapes still lack a clear reference, so this fee applies. Provide full hex codes and side/back images before we start to avoid this fee.
- **Platform:** VRChat on PC only. Quest/Android is not supported.
- **Supported apps:** VRChat, VSeeFace, Warudo, 3tene, VNyan. 3D models **cannot be used in VTube Studio** (it only supports Live2D).
- **Adding an app after the scope is confirmed** (for example, the scope was VRChat + VNyan and you later want Warudo) is charged separately based on the actual work involved. I will quote you before starting.
- **Performance Rank (VRChat):** the rank depends on how complex the design is. Detailed models (layered outfits, thick hair) may end up Poor or Very Poor, and players who block avatars by rank won't see the model fully. I will tell you the expected rank before setup so you can decide.
- **Tracking:** hand, finger, and face tracking accuracy depends on your app and devices (webcam, iPhone...), not on the model. ARKit/Perfect Sync face tracking (52 blendshapes) is **not offered at the moment**, as I currently have no way to test it.
- **Clipping:** with layered outfits, clothing may clip in some poses (especially with an app's default idle animation). I fix clipping in common poses but cannot guarantee every pose.
- **Custom animations are not included** (replacement idle, dances, emote animations...).
- **Outfits:** each package includes **1 outfit, with no outfit switching**. For another outfit, you need to order an outfit add-on (see Part II). I only make additional outfits for models I created.

## 5. Timeline

*(For standard models. More complex models, with multiple outfits or heavy PhysBones, may take longer; I will let you know in advance.)*

- Weeks 1–2: modeling and texturing
- Week 3: rigging and setup
- Week 4: buffer

**If you don't respond** (missing images, color codes, copyright confirmation...) **for more than 7 days**, the timeline is paused and extended by the waiting time. **If you don't respond for more than 30 days**, I may cancel the commission and keep the deposit as compensation for the time and work already done.

## 6. Payment and Revisions

- A 50% deposit is required before I start.
- **The remaining 50% is due as soon as I send the complete preview, before file delivery.** Source files are only sent after full payment.
- Payment methods: PayPal (international clients); bank transfer or MoMo (clients in Vietnam).
- Revisions: up to **3 free revisions**. Each revision is limited to small changes within the agreed design (colors, positions, proportions, expressions...). **Major changes** (new hairstyle, new outfit, adding/removing parts not in the original reference) count as **new requests outside the package scope** and are charged separately based on the actual work. They do not count toward the 3 free revisions.
- After the 3 free revisions: each additional revision costs **5% of the original price**.
- If you cancel the commission: I will keep the deposit as compensation for the time and effort already spent.
- If I cancel the commission: I will refund the full deposit.
- Technical errors found after delivery (e.g. incorrect weight painting, texture errors) are fixed for free. Changes or additions beyond technical errors are charged separately.
- Free technical fixes are available for **1 month** after delivery. This does not cover errors caused by edits made by you or a third party.

- You may use the model for **commercial purposes** (monetized streams, videos...).
- You **may not** resell, share, or redistribute the model files in any form.
- You may edit the model yourself or hire someone else to edit it after delivery.
- I may show images/videos of the model in my portfolio after you debut with it, or 3 months after delivery, whichever comes first.
