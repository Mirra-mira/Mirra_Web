# Game Model Terms of Service

## 1. Scope of Service

- 3D anime-style character models for games, built from your 2D character design.
- Scope: modeling, rigging, blendshapes. Delivered as FBX with textures.
- **Includes:**
  - Anime model, within your tris limit (default under 100k tris).
  - Humanoid rig for Unity, compatible with existing animations; FBX is test-imported into Unity before delivery.
  - Bones for hair and long cloth parts (skirts, capes…) so you can set up physics.
  - Separated head and hair for first-person games, hideable when the camera is inside the character's head.
  - Commercial usage rights (included in the price).
  - Basic blendshape pack: 5 mouth shapes a-i-u-e-o, blink, 4 expressions. A persistent expression can be set as the model's default face.
- **Not included:**
  - Animation.
  - Physics setup, colliders, shaders, prefabs, animator in the engine (handled on your side).
  - Character design from scratch (a 2D design is required).

## 2. Workflow

1. Client sends the character design and answers the booking questions.
2. I provide a final quote after reviewing the design.
3. 50% deposit to start.
4. Rough blockout → client approval. **After the blockout is approved,** details initially left to the artist are considered agreed-upon and will not count as free fixes outside the standard 3 revisions.
5. Finalize model and texture.
6. Rig and blendshapes.
7. Send preview, client pays the remaining 50%.
8. Deliver the FBX file.

## 3. Design and References

- A 2D design is required (as many angles as possible). This service does not include designing a character from scratch.
- When reference views contradict each other, **the front view is treated as canonical**. Unclear details are decided by the artist and approved by the client at the blockout stage (Step 4).
- **Missing views (back, side):** +15% surcharge, since the artist has to decide those parts.
- The client is **responsible for the usage rights of the original design**, including AI-generated designs. If the design infringes third-party copyright, the client bears the responsibility.

## 4. Timeline

- **Typical turnaround: about 3 weeks** from receiving the deposit and all materials.
- Progress updates **every Sunday**.
- The client's desired deadline is discussed during the quote stage; if a tighter deadline than 3 weeks is needed, I'll confirm feasibility before accepting the commission.

## 5. Payment, Revisions, Warranty

- **50% deposit** to start. **The remaining 50% is paid when I send the preview**, before file delivery.
- **Revisions:** 3 free revisions. Each subsequent revision costs **5% of the order value**.
- **Warranty:** technical issues (weight paint, texture, rig) are fixed free of charge within **1 month** of delivery.
- **Not covered by warranty:**
  - Physics setup, colliders, shaders, prefabs, animator in the engine on your side.
  - Model edits made by third parties after delivery.
- **Unilateral cancellation by the client: the deposit is non-refundable.**

## 6. Surcharges and Discounts

- **Design missing views (back, side):** +15% of the order value.
- **Extra expression beyond the pack:** $2 / expression.
- **Design more complex than the Complex tier** (see the pricing table): custom quote.
- **Returning customer, next character:** 5–10% discount.
- If the declared client category (Indie / Studio) does not match reality, I may adjust the price to the correct category.

## 7. For Studio Clients

Studio clients (a company, or a project backed by a publisher / investor) also get:

- **Contract** on request.
- **Model confidentiality** until the game is announced (basic NDA).
